Palworld Bellanoir Libero Breeding Combos

Bellanoir Libero cannot be bred from two different Pals. The only pair that produces it is Bellanoir Libero with another Bellanoir Libero, so the first one has to be caught in the wild — after that it breeds true.

How to breed Bellanoir Libero

Pair the two Pals in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box. Parent order never matters — Bellanoir Libero + Bellanoir Libero and Bellanoir Libero + Bellanoir Libero both hatch Bellanoir Libero.
