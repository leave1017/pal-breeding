250 parent pairs produce Incineram in Palworld 1.0. They are listed cheapest first, by the combined rarity of the two parents, so the easiest way to get one is at the top.

How to breed Incineram

Pair the two Pals in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box. Parent order never matters — Smokie + Wistella and Wistella + Smokie both hatch Incineram.
