How to get Lyleen Noct

The cheapest route to Lyleen Noct is Menasting + Lyleen, whose parents total 18 in combined rarity — the lowest of the 2 pairs that reach it. At rank 266 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

Kept as a parent, Lyleen Noct feeds into 72 different offspring, the pick of them Anubis, Menasting and Astegon — a broad range of lines, which is worth remembering before you release a spare.

Lyleen Noct is a pure Dark-type. Typing never decides what an egg becomes — a hidden breeding-power value does, and Lyleen Noct's is 220 on a 20–3,100 scale — but at rarity 10 it sits near the top of the scale, so most routes to it pass through other high-rarity parents.

One trap worth flagging: Lyleen Noct is a variant, a separate Paldeck entry from Lyleen, and shares none of its breeding pairs. Reading off Lyleen and assuming the pairs carry over is the usual mistake — only the pairs on this page hatch Lyleen Noct.

How to breed Lyleen Noct

To breed Lyleen Noct, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Menasting + Lyleen hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Menasting + Lyleen at a combined rarity of 18, shown near the top of the table.
