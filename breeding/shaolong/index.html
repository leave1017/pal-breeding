Palworld Shaolong Breeding Combos

Shaolong cannot be bred from two different Pals. The only pair that produces it is Shaolong with another Shaolong, so the first one has to be caught in the wild — after that it breeds true.

Paldex#192 Rarity9 ElementDragonWater Parent pairs1 Can produce70 Pals

Work suitabilityWatering 8Gathering 5

How to breed Shaolong

Pair the two Pals in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box. Parent order never matters — Shaolong + Shaolong and Shaolong + Shaolong both hatch Shaolong.
