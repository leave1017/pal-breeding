Palworld Blazamut Ryu Breeding Combos

Blazamut Ryu cannot be bred from two different Pals. The only pair that produces it is Blazamut Ryu with another Blazamut Ryu, so the first one has to be caught in the wild — after that it breeds true.

How to breed Blazamut Ryu

Pair the two Pals in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box. Parent order never matters — Blazamut Ryu + Blazamut Ryu and Blazamut Ryu + Blazamut Ryu both hatch Blazamut Ryu.
