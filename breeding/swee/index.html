Palworld Swee Breeding Combos

146 parent pairs produce Swee in Palworld 1.0. They are listed cheapest first, by the combined rarity of the two parents, so the easiest way to get one is at the top.

How to get Swee

Start with Cattiva + Daedream: at a combined rarity of 2 it is the lowest of the 146 pairs that reach it. Sitting at rank 128 of 299 for reachability, it comes from more pairs than the average Pal, so you very likely own a workable pair already.

Pair Swee across the roster and it produces 108 different Pals; the ones worth breeding for are Kingpaca, Elizabee and Petallia. That reach — an unusually wide range of results — is the case for keeping one on hand.

On the breeding graph, Swee is a pure Ice-type. What hatches from a pair is set by breeding power (Swee carries 2,840, within the 20–3,100 range) rather than element, and at rarity 1 it is common enough to serve as a cheap parent elsewhere.

How to breed Swee

To breed Swee, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Cattiva + Daedream hatches the same egg either way round. With more pairs than the average Pal leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Cattiva + Daedream at a combined rarity of 2, shown near the top of the table.
