Palworld Grizzbolt Breeding Combos

Grizzbolt cannot be bred from two different Pals. The only pair that produces it is Grizzbolt with another Grizzbolt, so the first one has to be caught in the wild — after that it breeds true.

How to get Grizzbolt

You will not hatch Grizzbolt from a mixed pair; breeding only reproduces it once you already own one. The first has to come from the wild, which is the whole cost — it breeds true from then on.

Pair Grizzbolt across the roster and it produces 88 different Pals; the ones worth breeding for are Menasting, Elizabee and Petallia. That reach — a broad range of results — is the case for keeping one on hand.

On the breeding graph, Grizzbolt is a pure Electric-type. What hatches from a pair is set by breeding power (Grizzbolt carries 1,020, within the 20–3,100 range) rather than element, and at rarity 8 it sits near the top of the scale, so most routes to it pass through other high-rarity parents.

How to breed Grizzbolt

Grizzbolt is the exception to the usual breeding advice: no mixed pair produces it. Put two Grizzbolt together in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box and the egg hatches another Grizzbolt, but there is no combination of other Pals that will — the first one has to be caught.
