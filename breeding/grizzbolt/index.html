Palworld Grizzbolt Breeding Combos

Grizzbolt cannot be bred from two different Pals. The only pair that produces it is Grizzbolt with another Grizzbolt, so the first one has to be caught in the wild — after that it breeds true.

How to breed Grizzbolt

Pair the two Pals in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box. Parent order never matters — Grizzbolt + Grizzbolt and Grizzbolt + Grizzbolt both hatch Grizzbolt.
