How to get Smokie Cryst

Start with Munchill + Smokie: at a combined rarity of 5 it is the least expensive of the 2 pairs that produce Smokie Cryst. At rank 258 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

Pair Smokie Cryst across the roster and it produces 98 different Pals; the ones worth breeding for are Menasting, Kingpaca and Elizabee. That reach — an unusually wide range of results — is the case for keeping one on hand.

On the breeding graph, Smokie Cryst is dual-typed, Dark and Ice, which puts it in the minority that carry two elements and makes it a useful bridge toward other dual-typed Pals. What hatches from a pair is set by breeding power (Smokie Cryst carries 1,610, within the 20–3,100 range) rather than element, and at rarity 3 it is common enough to serve as a cheap parent elsewhere.

Because Smokie Cryst is a variant form of Smokie, its combinations are entirely its own; the base Pal's pairs will not produce it. That mismatch catches people out constantly, so treat this page as the only source for Smokie Cryst.

How to breed Smokie Cryst

To breed Smokie Cryst, pair its two parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Munchill + Smokie hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Munchill + Smokie at a combined rarity of 5, shown at the top of the table.
