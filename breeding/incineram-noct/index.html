How to get Incineram Noct

Of the 2 pairs that hatch Incineram Noct, the easiest you can start from is Incineram + Maraith, a combined rarity of 10. At rank 228 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

As a parent Incineram Noct earns its box slot: it contributes to 90 distinct offspring, most usefully Elizabee, Petallia and Vaelet. Re-catching one later costs more than the slot it holds now.

On the breeding graph, Incineram Noct is a pure Dark-type. What hatches from a pair is set by breeding power (Incineram Noct carries 1,310, within the 20–3,100 range) rather than element, and at rarity 5 it sits mid-scale, cheap to breed freely yet still useful as a parent.

Because Incineram Noct is a variant form of Incineram, its combinations are entirely its own; the base Pal's pairs will not produce it. That mismatch catches people out constantly, so treat this page as the only source for Incineram Noct.

How to breed Incineram Noct

To breed Incineram Noct, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Incineram + Maraith hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Incineram + Maraith at a combined rarity of 10, shown near the top of the table.
