Palworld Orserk Breeding Combos

Orserk cannot be bred from two different Pals. The only pair that produces it is Orserk with another Orserk, so the first one has to be caught in the wild — after that it breeds true.

How to breed Orserk

Pair the two Pals in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box. Parent order never matters — Orserk + Orserk and Orserk + Orserk both hatch Orserk.
