How to get Quivern Botan

Of the 2 pairs that hatch Quivern Botan, the easiest is Quivern + Lullu, a combined rarity of 11. At rank 248 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

As a parent Quivern Botan earns its box slot: it contributes to 91 distinct offspring, most usefully Elizabee, Petallia and Vaelet. Re-catching one later costs more than the slot it holds now.

Quivern Botan is dual-typed, Dragon and Grass, which puts it in the minority that carry two elements and makes it a useful bridge toward other dual-typed Pals. Typing never decides what an egg becomes — a hidden breeding-power value does, and Quivern Botan's is 1,300 on a 20–3,100 scale — but at rarity 8 it sits near the top of the scale, so most routes to it pass through other high-rarity parents.

One trap worth flagging: Quivern Botan is a variant, a separate Paldeck entry from Quivern, and shares none of its breeding pairs. Reading off Quivern and assuming the pairs carry over is the usual mistake — only the pairs on this page hatch Quivern Botan.

How to breed Quivern Botan

To breed Quivern Botan, pair its two parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Quivern + Lullu hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Quivern + Lullu at a combined rarity of 11, shown at the top of the table.
