How to get Whalaska Ignis

Of the 2 pairs that hatch Whalaska Ignis, the easiest is Chillet Ignis + Whalaska, a combined rarity of 13. At rank 259 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

As a parent Whalaska Ignis earns its box slot: it contributes to 79 distinct offspring, most usefully Anubis, Menasting and Astegon. Re-catching one later costs more than the slot it holds now.

On the breeding graph, Whalaska Ignis is dual-typed, Ice and Fire, which puts it in the minority that carry two elements and makes it a useful bridge toward other dual-typed Pals. What hatches from a pair is set by breeding power (Whalaska Ignis carries 370, within the 20–3,100 range) rather than element, and at rarity 8 it sits near the top of the scale, so most routes to it pass through other high-rarity parents.

Because Whalaska Ignis is a variant form of Whalaska, its combinations are entirely its own; the base Pal's pairs will not produce it. That mismatch catches people out constantly, so treat this page as the only source for Whalaska Ignis.

How to breed Whalaska Ignis

To breed Whalaska Ignis, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Chillet Ignis + Whalaska hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Chillet Ignis + Whalaska at a combined rarity of 13, shown near the top of the table.
