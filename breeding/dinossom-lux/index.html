How to get Dinossom Lux

The cheapest route to Dinossom Lux is Dinossom + Rayhound, whose parents total 11 in combined rarity — the lowest of the 2 pairs that reach it. At rank 223 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

Kept as a parent, Dinossom Lux feeds into 99 different offspring, the pick of them Kingpaca, Elizabee and Petallia — an unusually wide range of lines, which is worth remembering before you release a spare.

Dinossom Lux is dual-typed, Electric and Dragon, which puts it in the minority that carry two elements and makes it a useful bridge toward other dual-typed Pals. Typing never decides what an egg becomes — a hidden breeding-power value does, and Dinossom Lux's is 1,890 on a 20–3,100 scale — but at rarity 7 it sits mid-scale, cheap to breed freely yet still useful as a parent.

One trap worth flagging: Dinossom Lux is a variant, a separate Paldeck entry from Dinossom, and shares none of its breeding pairs. Reading off Dinossom and assuming the pairs carry over is the usual mistake — only the pairs on this page hatch Dinossom Lux.

How to breed Dinossom Lux

To breed Dinossom Lux, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Dinossom + Rayhound hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Dinossom + Rayhound at a combined rarity of 11, shown near the top of the table.
