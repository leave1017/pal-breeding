How to get Snock Lux

Of the 2 pairs that hatch Snock Lux, the easiest of the 2 pairs is Turtacle Terra + Snock, a combined rarity of 6. At rank 263 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

As a parent Snock Lux earns its box slot: it contributes to 97 distinct offspring, most usefully Menasting, Kingpaca and Elizabee. Re-catching one later costs more than the slot it holds now.

On the breeding graph, Snock Lux is dual-typed, Electric and Ground, which puts it in the minority that carry two elements and makes it a useful bridge toward other dual-typed Pals. What hatches from a pair is set by breeding power (Snock Lux carries 1,500, within the 20–3,100 range) rather than element, and at rarity 3 it is common enough to serve as a cheap parent elsewhere.

Because Snock Lux is a variant form of Snock, its combinations are entirely its own; the base Pal's pairs will not produce it. That mismatch catches people out constantly, so treat this page as the only source for Snock Lux.

How to breed Snock Lux

To breed Snock Lux, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Turtacle Terra + Snock hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Turtacle Terra + Snock at a combined rarity of 6, shown near the top of the table.
