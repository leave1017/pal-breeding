How to get Rayhound Cryst

Start with Foxcicle + Rayhound: at a combined rarity of 10 it is the lowest of the 2 pairs that reach it. At rank 234 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

Pair Rayhound Cryst across the roster and it produces 96 different Pals; the ones worth breeding for are Menasting, Kingpaca and Elizabee. That reach — an unusually wide range of results — is the case for keeping one on hand.

Rayhound Cryst is a pure Ice-type. Typing never decides what an egg becomes — a hidden breeding-power value does, and Rayhound Cryst's is 1,600 on a 20–3,100 scale — but at rarity 6 it sits mid-scale, cheap to breed freely yet still useful as a parent.

One trap worth flagging: Rayhound Cryst is a variant, a separate Paldeck entry from Rayhound, and shares none of its breeding pairs. Reading off Rayhound and assuming the pairs carry over is the usual mistake — only the pairs on this page hatch Rayhound Cryst.

How to breed Rayhound Cryst

To breed Rayhound Cryst, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Foxcicle + Rayhound hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Foxcicle + Rayhound at a combined rarity of 10, shown near the top of the table.
