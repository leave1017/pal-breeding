How to get Dumud Gild

Of the 2 pairs that hatch Dumud Gild, the easiest is Eikthyrdeer Terra + Dumud, a combined rarity of 10. At rank 241 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

As a parent Dumud Gild earns its box slot: it contributes to 97 distinct offspring, most usefully Menasting, Kingpaca and Elizabee. Re-catching one later costs more than the slot it holds now.

On the breeding graph, Dumud Gild is dual-typed, Ground and Water, which puts it in the minority that carry two elements and makes it a useful bridge toward other dual-typed Pals. What hatches from a pair is set by breeding power (Dumud Gild carries 1,620, within the 20–3,100 range) rather than element, and at rarity 5 it sits mid-scale, cheap to breed freely yet still useful as a parent.

Because Dumud Gild is a variant form of Dumud, its combinations are entirely its own; the base Pal's pairs will not produce it. That mismatch catches people out constantly, so treat this page as the only source for Dumud Gild.

How to breed Dumud Gild

To breed Dumud Gild, pair its two parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Eikthyrdeer Terra + Dumud hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Eikthyrdeer Terra + Dumud at a combined rarity of 10, shown at the top of the table.
