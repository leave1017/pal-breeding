How to get Pierdon Cryst

Start with Pierdon + Wumpo: at a combined rarity of 13 it is the lowest of the 2 pairs that reach it. At rank 252 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

Pair Pierdon Cryst across the roster and it produces 85 different Pals; the ones worth breeding for are Anubis, Menasting and Astegon. That reach — a broad range of results — is the case for keeping one on hand.

On the breeding graph, Pierdon Cryst is a pure Ice-type. What hatches from a pair is set by breeding power (Pierdon Cryst carries 460, within the 20–3,100 range) rather than element, and at rarity 7 it sits mid-scale, cheap to breed freely yet still useful as a parent.

Because Pierdon Cryst is a variant form of Pierdon, its combinations are entirely its own; the base Pal's pairs will not produce it. That mismatch catches people out constantly, so treat this page as the only source for Pierdon Cryst.

How to breed Pierdon Cryst

To breed Pierdon Cryst, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Pierdon + Wumpo hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Pierdon + Wumpo at a combined rarity of 13, shown near the top of the table.
