Palworld Caprity Breeding Combos

110 parent pairs produce Caprity in Palworld 1.0. They are listed cheapest first, by the combined rarity of the two parents, so the easiest way to get one is at the top.

How to get Caprity

Of the 110 pairs that hatch Caprity, the easiest is Cattiva + Ribbuny Botan, a combined rarity of 2. Sitting at rank 140 of 299 for reachability, it comes from more pairs than the average Pal, so you very likely own a workable pair already.

As a parent Caprity earns its box slot: it contributes to 104 distinct offspring, most usefully Kingpaca, Elizabee and Petallia. Re-catching one later costs more than the slot it holds now.

Caprity is a pure Grass-type. Typing never decides what an egg becomes — a hidden breeding-power value does, and Caprity's is 2,610 on a 20–3,100 scale — but at rarity 3 it is common enough to serve as a cheap parent elsewhere.

How to breed Caprity

To breed Caprity, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Cattiva + Ribbuny Botan hatches the same egg either way round. With more pairs than the average Pal leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Cattiva + Ribbuny Botan at a combined rarity of 2, shown near the top of the table.
