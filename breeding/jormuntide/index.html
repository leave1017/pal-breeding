Palworld Jormuntide Breeding Combos

210 parent pairs produce Jormuntide in Palworld 1.0. They are listed cheapest first, by the combined rarity of the two parents, so the easiest way to get one is at the top.

How to get Jormuntide

Of the 210 pairs that hatch Jormuntide, the easiest is Fenglope Lux + Celesdir Noct, a combined rarity of 10. Sitting at rank 90 of 299 for reachability, it comes from an unusually large number of pairs, so you very likely own a workable pair already.

As a parent Jormuntide earns its box slot: it contributes to 82 distinct offspring, most usefully Anubis, Menasting and Suzaku Aqua. Re-catching one later costs more than the slot it holds now.

On the breeding graph, Jormuntide is dual-typed, Dragon and Water, which puts it in the minority that carry two elements and makes it a useful bridge toward other dual-typed Pals. What hatches from a pair is set by breeding power (Jormuntide carries 590, within the 20–3,100 range) rather than element, and at rarity 8 it sits near the top of the scale, so most routes to it pass through other high-rarity parents.

How to breed Jormuntide

To breed Jormuntide, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Fenglope Lux + Celesdir Noct hatches the same egg either way round. With an unusually large number of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Fenglope Lux + Celesdir Noct at a combined rarity of 10, shown near the top of the table.
