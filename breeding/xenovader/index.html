Palworld Xenovader Breeding Combos

Xenovader cannot be bred from two different Pals. The only pair that produces it is Xenovader with another Xenovader, so the first one has to be caught in the wild — after that it breeds true.

Paldex#145 Rarity7 ElementDark Parent pairs1 Can produce99 Pals

Work suitabilityLumbering 3Transporting 2

Always hatches withOtherworldly Cells

How to breed Xenovader

Pair the two Pals in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box. Parent order never matters — Xenovader + Xenovader and Xenovader + Xenovader both hatch Xenovader.
