How to get Wixen Noct

There is no breeding shortcut to a first Wixen Noct. It cannot be produced from two different Pals, so you have to catch one in the wild; after that, pairing it with another Wixen Noct breeds true and the line sustains itself.

Kept as a parent, Wixen Noct feeds into 95 different offspring, the pick of them Menasting, Kingpaca and Elizabee — an unusually wide range of lines, which is worth remembering before you release a spare.

Wixen Noct is dual-typed, Fire and Dark, which puts it in the minority that carry two elements and makes it a useful bridge toward other dual-typed Pals. Typing never decides what an egg becomes — a hidden breeding-power value does, and Wixen Noct's is 1,490 on a 20–3,100 scale — but at rarity 6 it sits mid-scale, cheap to breed freely yet still useful as a parent.

One trap worth flagging: Wixen Noct is a variant, a separate Paldeck entry from Wixen, and shares none of its breeding pairs. Reading off Wixen and assuming the pairs carry over is the usual mistake — only the pairs on this page hatch Wixen Noct.

How to breed Wixen Noct

Pair the two Pals in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box. Parent order never matters — Wixen Noct + Wixen Noct and Wixen Noct + Wixen Noct both hatch Wixen Noct.
