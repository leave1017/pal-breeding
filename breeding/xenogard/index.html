Palworld Xenogard Breeding Combos

Xenogard cannot be bred from two different Pals. The only pair that produces it is Xenogard with another Xenogard, so the first one has to be caught in the wild — after that it breeds true.

Paldex#146 Rarity9 ElementDragon Parent pairs1 Can produce89 Pals

Work suitabilityMining 4

How to breed Xenogard

Pair the two Pals in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box. Parent order never matters — Xenogard + Xenogard and Xenogard + Xenogard both hatch Xenogard.
