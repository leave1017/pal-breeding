How to get Robinquill Terra

Of the 2 pairs that hatch Robinquill Terra, the easiest is Fuddler + Robinquill, a combined rarity of 6. At rank 219 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

As a parent Robinquill Terra earns its box slot: it contributes to 98 distinct offspring, most usefully Menasting, Kingpaca and Elizabee. Re-catching one later costs more than the slot it holds now.

Robinquill Terra is dual-typed, Grass and Ground, which puts it in the minority that carry two elements and makes it a useful bridge toward other dual-typed Pals. Typing never decides what an egg becomes — a hidden breeding-power value does, and Robinquill Terra's is 2,050 on a 20–3,100 scale — but at rarity 6 it sits mid-scale, cheap to breed freely yet still useful as a parent.

One trap worth flagging: Robinquill Terra is a variant, a separate Paldeck entry from Robinquill, and shares none of its breeding pairs. Reading off Robinquill and assuming the pairs carry over is the usual mistake — only the pairs on this page hatch Robinquill Terra.

How to breed Robinquill Terra

To breed Robinquill Terra, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Fuddler + Robinquill hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Fuddler + Robinquill at a combined rarity of 6, shown near the top of the table.
