How to get Skutlass Ignis

Start with Gobfin Ignis + Skutlass: at a combined rarity of 8 it is the lowest of the 2 pairs that reach it. At rank 249 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

Pair Skutlass Ignis across the roster and it produces 96 different Pals; the ones worth breeding for are Menasting, Kingpaca and Elizabee. That reach — an unusually wide range of results — is the case for keeping one on hand.

Skutlass Ignis is dual-typed, Water and Fire, which puts it in the minority that carry two elements and makes it a useful bridge toward other dual-typed Pals. Typing never decides what an egg becomes — a hidden breeding-power value does, and Skutlass Ignis's is 1,590 on a 20–3,100 scale — but at rarity 5 it sits mid-scale, cheap to breed freely yet still useful as a parent.

One trap worth flagging: Skutlass Ignis is a variant, a separate Paldeck entry from Skutlass, and shares none of its breeding pairs. Reading off Skutlass and assuming the pairs carry over is the usual mistake — only the pairs on this page hatch Skutlass Ignis.

How to breed Skutlass Ignis

To breed Skutlass Ignis, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Gobfin Ignis + Skutlass hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Gobfin Ignis + Skutlass at a combined rarity of 8, shown near the top of the table.
