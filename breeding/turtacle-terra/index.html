How to get Turtacle Terra

Of the 2 pairs that hatch Turtacle Terra, the easiest is Turtacle + Digtoise, a combined rarity of 8. At rank 204 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

As a parent Turtacle Terra earns its box slot: it contributes to 90 distinct offspring, most usefully Menasting, Kingpaca and Elizabee. Re-catching one later costs more than the slot it holds now.

On the breeding graph, Turtacle Terra is dual-typed, Water and Ground, which puts it in the minority that carry two elements and makes it a useful bridge toward other dual-typed Pals. What hatches from a pair is set by breeding power (Turtacle Terra carries 1,330, within the 20–3,100 range) rather than element, and at rarity 4 it sits mid-scale, cheap to breed freely yet still useful as a parent.

Because Turtacle Terra is a variant form of Turtacle, its combinations are entirely its own; the base Pal's pairs will not produce it. That mismatch catches people out constantly, so treat this page as the only source for Turtacle Terra.

How to breed Turtacle Terra

To breed Turtacle Terra, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Turtacle + Digtoise hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Turtacle + Digtoise at a combined rarity of 8, shown near the top of the table.
