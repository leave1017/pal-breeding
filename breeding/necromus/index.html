Palworld Necromus Breeding Combos

Necromus cannot be bred from two different Pals. The only pair that produces it is Necromus with another Necromus, so the first one has to be caught in the wild — after that it breeds true.

How to get Necromus

Necromus is one of the Pals breeding cannot hand you from scratch — the only pair that yields it is two of its own kind. Catch the first, and every Necromus after that comes from the ranch.

As a parent Necromus earns its box slot: it contributes to 71 distinct offspring, most usefully Anubis, Menasting and Astegon. Re-catching one later costs more than the slot it holds now.

On the breeding graph, Necromus is a pure Dark-type. What hatches from a pair is set by breeding power (Necromus carries 190, within the 20–3,100 range) rather than element, and at rarity 20 it sits near the top of the scale, so most routes to it pass through other high-rarity parents.

How to breed Necromus

Necromus is the exception to the usual breeding advice: no mixed pair produces it. Put two Necromus together in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box and the egg hatches another Necromus, but there is no combination of other Pals that will — the first one has to be caught.
