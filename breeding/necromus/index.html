Palworld Necromus Breeding Combos

Necromus cannot be bred from two different Pals. The only pair that produces it is Necromus with another Necromus, so the first one has to be caught in the wild — after that it breeds true.

Paldex#199 Rarity20 ElementDark Parent pairs1 Can produce72 Pals

Work suitabilityLumbering 6Mining 6

How to breed Necromus

Pair the two Pals in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box. Parent order never matters — Necromus + Necromus and Necromus + Necromus both hatch Necromus.
