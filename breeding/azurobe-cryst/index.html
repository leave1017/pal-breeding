How to get Azurobe Cryst

Start with Azurobe + Frostplume: at a combined rarity of 11 it is the lowest of the 2 pairs that reach it. At rank 207 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

Pair Azurobe Cryst across the roster and it produces 90 different Pals; the ones worth breeding for are Menasting, Elizabee and Petallia. That reach — an unusually wide range of results — is the case for keeping one on hand.

On the breeding graph, Azurobe Cryst is dual-typed, Ice and Dragon, which puts it in the minority that carry two elements and makes it a useful bridge toward other dual-typed Pals. What hatches from a pair is set by breeding power (Azurobe Cryst carries 1,220, within the 20–3,100 range) rather than element, and at rarity 8 it sits near the top of the scale, so most routes to it pass through other high-rarity parents.

Because Azurobe Cryst is a variant form of Azurobe, its combinations are entirely its own; the base Pal's pairs will not produce it. That mismatch catches people out constantly, so treat this page as the only source for Azurobe Cryst.

How to breed Azurobe Cryst

To breed Azurobe Cryst, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Azurobe + Frostplume hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Azurobe + Frostplume at a combined rarity of 11, shown near the top of the table.
