How to get Gloopie Primo

The cheapest route to Gloopie Primo is Gloopie + Valentail, whose parents total 7 in combined rarity — the lowest of the 2 pairs that reach it. At rank 209 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

Kept as a parent, Gloopie Primo feeds into 97 different offspring, the pick of them Menasting, Kingpaca and Elizabee — an unusually wide range of lines, which is worth remembering before you release a spare.

Gloopie Primo is dual-typed, Water and Neutral, which puts it in the minority that carry two elements and makes it a useful bridge toward other dual-typed Pals. Typing never decides what an egg becomes — a hidden breeding-power value does, and Gloopie Primo's is 1,630 on a 20–3,100 scale — but at rarity 6 it sits mid-scale, cheap to breed freely yet still useful as a parent.

One trap worth flagging: Gloopie Primo is a variant, a separate Paldeck entry from Gloopie, and shares none of its breeding pairs. Reading off Gloopie and assuming the pairs carry over is the usual mistake — only the pairs on this page hatch Gloopie Primo.

How to breed Gloopie Primo

To breed Gloopie Primo, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Gloopie + Valentail hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Gloopie + Valentail at a combined rarity of 7, shown near the top of the table.
