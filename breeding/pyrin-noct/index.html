How to get Pyrin Noct

The cheapest route to Pyrin Noct is Katress + Pyrin, whose parents total 12 in combined rarity — the lowest of the 2 pairs that reach it. At rank 230 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

Kept as a parent, Pyrin Noct feeds into 99 different offspring, the pick of them Menasting, Kingpaca and Elizabee — an unusually wide range of lines, which is worth remembering before you release a spare.

On the breeding graph, Pyrin Noct is dual-typed, Fire and Dark, which puts it in the minority that carry two elements and makes it a useful bridge toward other dual-typed Pals. What hatches from a pair is set by breeding power (Pyrin Noct carries 1,870, within the 20–3,100 range) rather than element, and at rarity 7 it sits mid-scale, cheap to breed freely yet still useful as a parent.

Because Pyrin Noct is a variant form of Pyrin, its combinations are entirely its own; the base Pal's pairs will not produce it. That mismatch catches people out constantly, so treat this page as the only source for Pyrin Noct.

How to breed Pyrin Noct

To breed Pyrin Noct, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Katress + Pyrin hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Katress + Pyrin at a combined rarity of 12, shown near the top of the table.
