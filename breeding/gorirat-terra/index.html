How to get Gorirat Terra

Start with Gorirat + Kikit: at a combined rarity of 9 it is the least expensive of the 2 pairs that produce Gorirat Terra. At rank 217 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

Pair Gorirat Terra across the roster and it produces 100 different Pals; the ones worth breeding for are Menasting, Kingpaca and Elizabee. That reach — an unusually wide range of results — is the case for keeping one on hand.

Gorirat Terra is a pure Ground-type. Typing never decides what an egg becomes — a hidden breeding-power value does, and Gorirat Terra's is 1,880 on a 20–3,100 scale — but at rarity 5 it sits mid-scale, cheap to breed freely yet still useful as a parent.

One trap worth flagging: Gorirat Terra is a variant, a separate Paldeck entry from Gorirat, and shares none of its breeding pairs. Reading off Gorirat and assuming the pairs carry over is the usual mistake — only the pairs on this page hatch Gorirat Terra.

How to breed Gorirat Terra

To breed Gorirat Terra, pair its two parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Gorirat + Kikit hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Gorirat + Kikit at a combined rarity of 9, shown at the top of the table.
