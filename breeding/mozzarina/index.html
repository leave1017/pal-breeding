Palworld Mozzarina Breeding Combos

81 parent pairs produce Mozzarina in Palworld 1.0. They are listed cheapest first, by the combined rarity of the two parents, so the easiest way to get one is at the top.

Work suitabilityFarming 2

How to get Mozzarina

Of the 81 pairs that hatch Mozzarina, the easiest is Cattiva + Swee, a combined rarity of 2. At rank 157 of 299 for reachability it comes from a moderate number of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

As a parent Mozzarina earns its box slot: it contributes to 107 distinct offspring, most usefully Kingpaca, Elizabee and Petallia. Re-catching one later costs more than the slot it holds now.

Mozzarina is a pure Neutral-type. Typing never decides what an egg becomes — a hidden breeding-power value does, and Mozzarina's is 2,800 on a 20–3,100 scale — but at rarity 2 it is common enough to serve as a cheap parent elsewhere.

How to breed Mozzarina

To breed Mozzarina, pair its two parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Cattiva + Swee hatches the same egg either way round. With a moderate number of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Cattiva + Swee at a combined rarity of 2, shown at the top of the table.
