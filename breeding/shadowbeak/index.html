Palworld Shadowbeak Breeding Combos

Shadowbeak cannot be bred from two different Pals. The only pair that produces it is Shadowbeak with another Shadowbeak, so the first one has to be caught in the wild — after that it breeds true.

How to get Shadowbeak

There is no breeding shortcut to a first Shadowbeak. It cannot be produced from two different Pals, so you have to catch one in the wild; after that, pairing it with another Shadowbeak breeds true and the line sustains itself.

Kept as a parent, Shadowbeak feeds into 81 different offspring, the pick of them Anubis, Menasting and Astegon — a broad range of lines, which is worth remembering before you release a spare.

On the breeding graph, Shadowbeak is a pure Dark-type. What hatches from a pair is set by breeding power (Shadowbeak carries 550, within the 20–3,100 range) rather than element, and at rarity 10 it sits near the top of the scale, so most routes to it pass through other high-rarity parents.

How to breed Shadowbeak

Pair the two Pals in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box. Parent order never matters — Shadowbeak + Shadowbeak and Shadowbeak + Shadowbeak both hatch Shadowbeak.
