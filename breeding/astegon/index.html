How to get Astegon

Start with Fenglope Lux + Silvance: at a combined rarity of 11 it is the lowest of the 104 pairs that reach it. Sitting at rank 146 of 299 for reachability, it comes from more pairs than the average Pal, so you very likely own a workable pair already.

Pair Astegon across the roster and it produces 81 different Pals; the ones worth breeding for are Anubis, Menasting and Knocklem. That reach — a broad range of results — is the case for keeping one on hand.

Astegon is dual-typed, Dragon and Dark, which puts it in the minority that carry two elements and makes it a useful bridge toward other dual-typed Pals. Typing never decides what an egg becomes — a hidden breeding-power value does, and Astegon's is 490 on a 20–3,100 scale — but at rarity 9 it sits near the top of the scale, so most routes to it pass through other high-rarity parents.

How to breed Astegon

To breed Astegon, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Fenglope Lux + Silvance hatches the same egg either way round. With more pairs than the average Pal leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Fenglope Lux + Silvance at a combined rarity of 11, shown near the top of the table.
