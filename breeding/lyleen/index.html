Palworld Lyleen Breeding Combos

Lyleen cannot be bred from two different Pals. The only pair that produces it is Lyleen with another Lyleen, so the first one has to be caught in the wild — after that it breeds true.

How to breed Lyleen

Pair the two Pals in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box. Parent order never matters — Lyleen + Lyleen and Lyleen + Lyleen both hatch Lyleen.
