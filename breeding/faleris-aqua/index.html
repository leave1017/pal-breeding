How to get Faleris Aqua

Start with Jormuntide + Faleris: at a combined rarity of 17 it is the lowest of the 2 pairs that reach it. At rank 267 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

Pair Faleris Aqua across the roster and it produces 84 different Pals; the ones worth breeding for are Anubis, Menasting and Astegon. That reach — a broad range of results — is the case for keeping one on hand.

Faleris Aqua is a pure Water-type. Typing never decides what an egg becomes — a hidden breeding-power value does, and Faleris Aqua's is 450 on a 20–3,100 scale — but at rarity 9 it sits near the top of the scale, so most routes to it pass through other high-rarity parents.

One trap worth flagging: Faleris Aqua is a variant, a separate Paldeck entry from Faleris, and shares none of its breeding pairs. Reading off Faleris and assuming the pairs carry over is the usual mistake — only the pairs on this page hatch Faleris Aqua.

How to breed Faleris Aqua

To breed Faleris Aqua, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Jormuntide + Faleris hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Jormuntide + Faleris at a combined rarity of 17, shown near the top of the table.
