Palworld Mimog Breeding Combos

Mimog cannot be bred from two different Pals. The only pair that produces it is Mimog with another Mimog, so the first one has to be caught in the wild — after that it breeds true.

Paldex#144 Rarity7 ElementNeutral Parent pairs1 Can produce99 Pals

Work suitabilityGathering 1Transporting 2

How to breed Mimog

Pair the two Pals in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box. Parent order never matters — Mimog + Mimog and Mimog + Mimog both hatch Mimog.
