Palworld Katress Ignis Breeding Combos

Katress Ignis cannot be bred from two different Pals. The only pair that produces it is Katress Ignis with another Katress Ignis, so the first one has to be caught in the wild — after that it breeds true.

How to breed Katress Ignis

Pair the two Pals in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box. Parent order never matters — Katress Ignis + Katress Ignis and Katress Ignis + Katress Ignis both hatch Katress Ignis.
