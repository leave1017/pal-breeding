How to get Helzephyr Lux

Start with Helzephyr + Beakon: at a combined rarity of 13 it is the least expensive of the 2 pairs that produce Helzephyr Lux. At rank 220 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

Pair Helzephyr Lux across the roster and it produces 90 different Pals; the ones worth breeding for are Menasting, Astegon and Elizabee. That reach — an unusually wide range of results — is the case for keeping one on hand.

Helzephyr Lux is dual-typed, Dark and Electric, which puts it in the minority that carry two elements and makes it a useful bridge toward other dual-typed Pals. Typing never decides what an egg becomes — a hidden breeding-power value does, and Helzephyr Lux's is 960 on a 20–3,100 scale — but at rarity 8 it sits near the top of the scale, so most routes to it pass through other high-rarity parents.

One trap worth flagging: Helzephyr Lux is a variant, a separate Paldeck entry from Helzephyr, and shares none of its breeding pairs. Reading off Helzephyr and assuming the pairs carry over is the usual mistake — only the pairs on this page hatch Helzephyr Lux.

How to breed Helzephyr Lux

To breed Helzephyr Lux, pair its two parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Helzephyr + Beakon hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Helzephyr + Beakon at a combined rarity of 13, shown at the top of the table.
