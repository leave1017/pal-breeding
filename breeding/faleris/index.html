Palworld Faleris Breeding Combos

Faleris cannot be bred from two different Pals. The only pair that produces it is Faleris with another Faleris, so the first one has to be caught in the wild — after that it breeds true.

Paldex#188 Rarity9 ElementFire Parent pairs1 Can produce86 Pals

Work suitabilityKindling 6Transporting 3

How to breed Faleris

Pair the two Pals in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box. Parent order never matters — Faleris + Faleris and Faleris + Faleris both hatch Faleris.
