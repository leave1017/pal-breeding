How to get Solmora Lux

Of the 2 pairs that hatch Solmora Lux, the easiest of the 2 pairs is Slowatt + Solmora, a combined rarity of 8. At rank 264 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

As a parent Solmora Lux earns its box slot: it contributes to 89 distinct offspring, most usefully Menasting, Elizabee and Petallia. Re-catching one later costs more than the slot it holds now.

On the breeding graph, Solmora Lux is dual-typed, Water and Electric, which puts it in the minority that carry two elements and makes it a useful bridge toward other dual-typed Pals. What hatches from a pair is set by breeding power (Solmora Lux carries 1,000, within the 20–3,100 range) rather than element, and at rarity 5 it sits mid-scale, cheap to breed freely yet still useful as a parent.

Because Solmora Lux is a variant form of Solmora, its combinations are entirely its own; the base Pal's pairs will not produce it. That mismatch catches people out constantly, so treat this page as the only source for Solmora Lux.

How to breed Solmora Lux

To breed Solmora Lux, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Slowatt + Solmora hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Slowatt + Solmora at a combined rarity of 8, shown near the top of the table.
