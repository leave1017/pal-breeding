How to get Neptilius

There is no breeding shortcut to a first Neptilius. It cannot be produced from two different Pals, so you have to catch one in the wild; after that, pairing it with another Neptilius breeds true and the line sustains itself.

Kept as a parent, Neptilius feeds into 71 different offspring, the pick of them Anubis, Menasting and Astegon — a broad range of lines, which is worth remembering before you release a spare.

On the breeding graph, Neptilius is a pure Water-type. What hatches from a pair is set by breeding power (Neptilius carries 160, within the 20–3,100 range) rather than element, and at rarity 20 it sits near the top of the scale, so most routes to it pass through other high-rarity parents.

How to breed Neptilius

Neptilius is the exception to the usual breeding advice: no mixed pair produces it. Put two Neptilius together in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box and the egg hatches another Neptilius, but there is no combination of other Pals that will — the first one has to be caught.
