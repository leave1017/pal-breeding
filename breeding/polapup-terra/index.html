How to get Polapup Terra

The cheapest route to Polapup Terra is Polapup + Surfent Terra, whose parents total 10 in combined rarity — the lowest of the 2 pairs that reach it. At rank 215 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

Kept as a parent, Polapup Terra feeds into 90 different offspring, the pick of them Menasting, Elizabee and Petallia — an unusually wide range of lines, which is worth remembering before you release a spare.

Polapup Terra is dual-typed, Ice and Ground, which puts it in the minority that carry two elements and makes it a useful bridge toward other dual-typed Pals. Typing never decides what an egg becomes — a hidden breeding-power value does, and Polapup Terra's is 1,100 on a 20–3,100 scale — but at rarity 6 it sits mid-scale, cheap to breed freely yet still useful as a parent.

One trap worth flagging: Polapup Terra is a variant, a separate Paldeck entry from Polapup, and shares none of its breeding pairs. Reading off Polapup and assuming the pairs carry over is the usual mistake — only the pairs on this page hatch Polapup Terra.

How to breed Polapup Terra

To breed Polapup Terra, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Polapup + Surfent Terra hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Polapup + Surfent Terra at a combined rarity of 10, shown near the top of the table.
