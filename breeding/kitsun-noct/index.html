How to get Kitsun Noct

The cheapest route to Kitsun Noct is Kitsun + Nyafia, whose parents total 10 in combined rarity — the lowest of the 2 pairs that reach it. At rank 242 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

Kept as a parent, Kitsun Noct feeds into 84 different offspring, the pick of them Anubis, Menasting and Astegon — a broad range of lines, which is worth remembering before you release a spare.

On the breeding graph, Kitsun Noct is a pure Dark-type. What hatches from a pair is set by breeding power (Kitsun Noct carries 800, within the 20–3,100 range) rather than element, and at rarity 6 it sits mid-scale, cheap to breed freely yet still useful as a parent.

Because Kitsun Noct is a variant form of Kitsun, its combinations are entirely its own; the base Pal's pairs will not produce it. That mismatch catches people out constantly, so treat this page as the only source for Kitsun Noct.

How to breed Kitsun Noct

To breed Kitsun Noct, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Kitsun + Nyafia hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Kitsun + Nyafia at a combined rarity of 10, shown near the top of the table.
