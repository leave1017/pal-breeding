How to get Celaray Lux

Of the 2 pairs that hatch Celaray Lux, the easiest to start from is Celaray + Univolt, a combined rarity of 8. At rank 192 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

As a parent Celaray Lux earns its box slot: it contributes to 101 distinct offspring, most usefully Kingpaca, Elizabee and Petallia. Re-catching one later costs more than the slot it holds now.

On the breeding graph, Celaray Lux is dual-typed, Water and Electric, which puts it in the minority that carry two elements and makes it a useful bridge toward other dual-typed Pals. What hatches from a pair is set by breeding power (Celaray Lux carries 2,380, within the 20–3,100 range) rather than element, and at rarity 4 it sits mid-scale, cheap to breed freely yet still useful as a parent.

Because Celaray Lux is a variant form of Celaray, its combinations are entirely its own; the base Pal's pairs will not produce it. That mismatch catches people out constantly, so treat this page as the only source for Celaray Lux.

How to breed Celaray Lux

To breed Celaray Lux, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Celaray + Univolt hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Celaray + Univolt at a combined rarity of 8, shown near the top of the table.
