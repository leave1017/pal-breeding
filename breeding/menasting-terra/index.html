How to get Menasting Terra

The cheapest route to Menasting Terra is Menasting + Knocklem, whose parents total 18 in combined rarity — the lowest of the 2 pairs that reach it. At rank 235 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

Kept as a parent, Menasting Terra feeds into 81 different offspring, the pick of them Anubis, Menasting and Blazamut — a broad range of lines, which is worth remembering before you release a spare.

On the breeding graph, Menasting Terra is a pure Ground-type. What hatches from a pair is set by breeding power (Menasting Terra carries 640, within the 20–3,100 range) rather than element, and at rarity 10 it sits near the top of the scale, so most routes to it pass through other high-rarity parents.

Because Menasting Terra is a variant form of Menasting, its combinations are entirely its own; the base Pal's pairs will not produce it. That mismatch catches people out constantly, so treat this page as the only source for Menasting Terra.

How to breed Menasting Terra

To breed Menasting Terra, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Menasting + Knocklem hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Menasting + Knocklem at a combined rarity of 18, shown near the top of the table.
