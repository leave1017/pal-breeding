How to get Killamari Primo

The cheapest route to Killamari Primo is Killamari + Ribbuny, whose parents total 2 in combined rarity — the lowest of the 2 pairs that reach it. At rank 201 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

Kept as a parent, Killamari Primo feeds into 105 different offspring, the pick of them Kingpaca, Elizabee and Petallia — an unusually wide range of lines, which is worth remembering before you release a spare.

Killamari Primo is dual-typed, Neutral and Water, which puts it in the minority that carry two elements and makes it a useful bridge toward other dual-typed Pals. Typing never decides what an egg becomes — a hidden breeding-power value does, and Killamari Primo's is 2,540 on a 20–3,100 scale — but at rarity 2 it is common enough to serve as a cheap parent elsewhere.

One trap worth flagging: Killamari Primo is a variant, a separate Paldeck entry from Killamari, and shares none of its breeding pairs. Reading off Killamari and assuming the pairs carry over is the usual mistake — only the pairs on this page hatch Killamari Primo.

How to breed Killamari Primo

To breed Killamari Primo, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Killamari + Ribbuny hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Killamari + Ribbuny at a combined rarity of 2, shown near the top of the table.
