How to get Relaxaurus Lux

Of the 2 pairs that hatch Relaxaurus Lux, the easiest is Sparkit + Relaxaurus, a combined rarity of 9. At rank 231 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

As a parent Relaxaurus Lux earns its box slot: it contributes to 82 distinct offspring, most usefully Anubis, Menasting and Astegon. Re-catching one later costs more than the slot it holds now.

Relaxaurus Lux is dual-typed, Dragon and Electric, which puts it in the minority that carry two elements and makes it a useful bridge toward other dual-typed Pals. Typing never decides what an egg becomes — a hidden breeding-power value does, and Relaxaurus Lux's is 770 on a 20–3,100 scale — but at rarity 9 it sits near the top of the scale, so most routes to it pass through other high-rarity parents.

One trap worth flagging: Relaxaurus Lux is a variant, a separate Paldeck entry from Relaxaurus, and shares none of its breeding pairs. Reading off Relaxaurus and assuming the pairs carry over is the usual mistake — only the pairs on this page hatch Relaxaurus Lux.

How to breed Relaxaurus Lux

To breed Relaxaurus Lux, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Sparkit + Relaxaurus hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Sparkit + Relaxaurus at a combined rarity of 9, shown near the top of the table.
