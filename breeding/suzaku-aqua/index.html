How to get Suzaku Aqua

Start with Jormuntide + Suzaku: at a combined rarity of 16 it is the lowest of the 2 pairs that reach it. At rank 247 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

Pair Suzaku Aqua across the roster and it produces 81 different Pals; the ones worth breeding for are Anubis, Menasting and Astegon. That reach — a broad range of results — is the case for keeping one on hand.

Suzaku Aqua is a pure Water-type. Typing never decides what an egg becomes — a hidden breeding-power value does, and Suzaku Aqua's is 740 on a 20–3,100 scale — but at rarity 9 it sits near the top of the scale, so most routes to it pass through other high-rarity parents.

One trap worth flagging: Suzaku Aqua is a variant, a separate Paldeck entry from Suzaku, and shares none of its breeding pairs. Reading off Suzaku and assuming the pairs carry over is the usual mistake — only the pairs on this page hatch Suzaku Aqua.

How to breed Suzaku Aqua

To breed Suzaku Aqua, pair its parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Jormuntide + Suzaku hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Jormuntide + Suzaku at a combined rarity of 16, shown near the top of the table.
