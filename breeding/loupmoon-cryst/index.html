How to get Loupmoon Cryst

Start with Loupmoon Cryst + Loupmoon Cryst: at a combined rarity of 6 it is the least expensive of the 2 pairs that produce Loupmoon Cryst. At rank 212 of 299 for reachability it comes from only a handful of pairs, so it is worth starting from the cheapest row rather than a longer chain.

Pair Loupmoon Cryst across the roster and it produces 89 different Pals; the ones worth breeding for are Elizabee, Petallia and Vaelet. That reach — a broad range of results — is the case for keeping one on hand.

Loupmoon Cryst is a pure Ice-type. Typing never decides what an egg becomes — a hidden breeding-power value does, and Loupmoon Cryst's is 1,290 on a 20–3,100 scale — but at rarity 3 it is common enough to serve as a cheap parent elsewhere.

One trap worth flagging: Loupmoon Cryst is a variant, a separate Paldeck entry from Loupmoon, and shares none of its breeding pairs. Reading off Loupmoon and assuming the pairs carry over is the usual mistake — only the pairs on this page hatch Loupmoon Cryst.

How to breed Loupmoon Cryst

To breed Loupmoon Cryst, pair its two parents in a Breeding Farm with cake in the feed box; parent order never changes the result, so Loupmoon Cryst + Loupmoon Cryst hatches the same egg either way round. With only a handful of pairs leading to it, the practical question is which pair is cheapest — Loupmoon Cryst + Loupmoon Cryst at a combined rarity of 6, shown at the top of the table.
