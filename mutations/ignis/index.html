How to breed a Ignis Pal

A variant is bred like any other Pal: pick it as the target and the calculator returns the pairs that produce it. The pairs belong to the variant, not to the base Pal. Kelpsea Ignis comes from 207 pairs, while Katress Ignis comes from 1. Open any Pal above to see them listed cheapest first.
