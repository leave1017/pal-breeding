How to breed a Noct Pal

A variant is bred like any other Pal: pick it as the target and the calculator returns the pairs that produce it. The pairs belong to the variant, not to the base Pal. Shroomer Noct comes from 501 pairs, while Wixen Noct comes from 1. Open any Pal above to see them listed cheapest first.
